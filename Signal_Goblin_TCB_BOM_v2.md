# Legacy BOM v2

This BOM describes an earlier Signal Goblin generation and is **not the Rev A source of truth**.

The authoritative Rev A BOM is:

- `hardware/rev_a/bom/Signal_Goblin_Rev_A_BOM.md`
- `hardware/rev_a/bom/Signal_Goblin_Rev_A_BOM.csv`

Legacy generations may contain STM32WB55CCU6, ESP32-C5-WROOM-1, integrated ILI9488/XPT2046 display assumptions, and alternative power components. Those choices are intentionally not carried forward as Rev A requirements.

The historical BOM content remains available in Git history.
